# Key Findings

1. **Summer was the busiest season.** The network recorded 103,369 attraction-to-attraction transitions in summer, 47.9% more than winter (69,910).
2. **Winter mobility was more concentrated.** The top 100 attractions captured 29.2% of winter weighted flow, compared with 23.1% in the least concentrated season.
3. **Lijiang Ancient City was the strongest hub.** It recorded a reconstructed annual weighted flow of 6,476, ahead of Dali Ancient City.
4. **A small group of destinations shaped a large share of observed movement.** The top 100 attractions accounted for 23.9% of full-network weighted flow.

These are descriptive patterns in travel-blog data, not population estimates. The source overrepresents people who publish travel blogs and popular destinations.
